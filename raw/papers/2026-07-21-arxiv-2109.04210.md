## arxiv:2109.04210
**Relevance to thesis:** HIGH – The L1 adaptive control law decouples adaptation from robustness, directly challenging the project’s MRAC inner loop and offering a computationally light candidate for handling severe mass/inertia mismatches (Priority 1).

**Contribution:** Cascades an L1 adaptive controller with NMPC to compensate model uncertainties and aerodynamic disturbances online. Achieves >90 % tracking error reduction under 60 % mass mismatch, wind, and slung payloads, with <5 % computational overhead.

**Method:** L1 inner loop uses a discrete-time state observer and adaptation law that converges at the rotor‑thrust level, enforcing the quadrotor to behave as the nominal NMPC model predicts. No online retraining; adapts to both parametric and non‑parametric uncertainties.

**Key results:**
- 90 % tracking error reduction versus non‑adaptive NMPC under unknown payloads up to 60 % mass and wind (p2).
- Rapidly rejects payload disturbances (e.g., 450 g) and maintains tracking under aerodynamic fan disturbances (p6).
- Outperforms INDI‑NMPC and GP‑MPC by >90 % under unknown payloads and disturbances (p7).
- Adaptation law executes in 10 µs with minimal overhead, cascaded to NMPC (p7).
- Robust to inertia and rotor‑arm length uncertainties (p5); 44 % error reduction with unknown slung payload (p6).

**Relevant to YOUR work (with pages):**
- L1’s decoupling of adaptation and robustness removes the gain‑tuning difficulties of the current MRAC; it could replace the per‑axis adaptive law with guaranteed transient performance (p1, p8) – Priority 1.
- Compensates unmodeled aerodynamics in real time without prior models (p2), directly supporting the project’s need to handle larger rate‑loop mismatches (Priority 1).
- Discrete‑time L1 observer and adaptation law (p4) run in 10 µs and <5 % overhead (p1, p7), making implementation on the STM32F4 FreeRTOS stack feasible (Priorities 1, 8).
- Requires no online model retraining and handles both parametric and non‑parametric disturbances (p7), a path to robust sim‑to‑real transfer without exact plant models (Priority 6).
- Enforces the plant to fly like the NMPC model (p8), analogous to the MRAC reference‑model idea, while the robustness guarantees (p8) inform safe adaptation bounds for safety‑critical firmware (Priority 8).

**How to apply / next step:**
- Implement the L1 adaptive law in the Phase‑2 Gazebo simulation and compare head‑to‑head with the current MRAC under mass/inertia mismatch and wind‑gust scenarios.
- Port the discrete‑time L1 observer (p4) to the STM32F4, verifying the claimed 10 µs execution, and integrate it with the existing rate‑loop on FreeRTOS.
- Use the L1 transient‑performance guarantees (p8) to derive a bound on actuator constraint violations (limitation p7) and define a safe operating envelope for flight tests.

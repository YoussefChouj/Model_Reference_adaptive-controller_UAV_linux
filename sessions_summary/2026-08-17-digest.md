# Session digest — 2026-08-17

**Topic:** User asked whether `ccc` (CocoIndex) and `graphify` are still valuable
for agents and, if so, should they be restored.

## Decision

Restore both. Spec at `.agent_contracts/agent-restore-ccc-and-graphify/spec.md`.

## Why restoring

- The rules mandate `ccc search` and `graphify-out/GRAPH_REPORT.md` as step 1
  and step 2 of every agent task (`.cursor/rules/000-core.mdc` §1).
- Both tools are partly broken today: `ccc` is uninstalled, `graphify` has a
  stale May 2025 artefact but no runnable pipeline, the knowledge-gate hook is
  empty-stub theatre.

## Embedder choice

**`qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b` via OpenRouter.** MTEB-Code 80.68, $0.01/M tokens
through DeepInfra, 32k context, Apache-2.0. Avoids the local-torch download
that previously caused two Linux crashes (per `000-core.mdc` §1b).

Rejected: voyage-code-3/4 (proprietary, 12× the cost for similar accuracy),
BGE-M3 (~63 MTEB-Code), local Qwen3-8B (16 GB disk).

## Critical principle the spec preserves

**Embeddings are layer 3, ripgrep is layer 1.** From the deep-research:

> Boris Cherny (Anthropic, May 2025): "agentic search outperformed everything
> by a lot" — exact symbol matching beats fuzzy similarity for code.
> CoREB benchmark: embeddings are near-zero ndcg@10 on short keyword queries
> like "auth flow" or "user service".

The spec positions `ccc` as a complement to grep, not a replacement. The
knowledge-stack protocol's ordering (ccc → graphify → wiki → decisions →
interfaces → lessons) is correct; only the enforcement is broken.

## Estimated cost

$0.45/month at 500 queries/day (per the deep-research report).
